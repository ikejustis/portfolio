About

As a design thinker and product builder with 6 years of experience, I craft digital experiences that merge people’s needs with real business impact.

My design philosophy centers on empathy and a deep exploration of context, driven by a fascination with human behavior and the unspoken motivations and pain points that shape how people use technology.

I believe in building thoughtful, scalable and inclusive products that don’t just solve problems, but genuinely connect with people and improve their well-being. My background in the arts sharpens this perspective: music, animation and short films inform how I think, solve problems and bring ideas to life. My goal is always to create experiences that are intuitive and deeply resonant.
