ABOUT REEMA

Music, for her, is storytelling.

Two years behind the decks have made her a familiar current in Los Angeles nightlife. Every set is built with intention: a pulse that starts slow, then blooms.

Her sound carries every city that raised her: the tempo of Dubai nights, the warmth of Kuala Lumpur, the grit and soul of DC, the open energy of LA.
